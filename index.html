About Us & Our App

We are dedicated to improving the environment by planting trees in cities, beginning with Miami. Our mission is to create a greener, healthier Miami for all residents and visitors. We believe in the power of community and the importance of engaging with residents, businesses and government to make a lasting impact. By working together, we can transform urban spaces into lush, green environments that provide numerous benefits for both people and wildlife. Moreover, by connecting schools, parks, shops, and homes with tree lined streets, we can make walking, biking, driving and using public transit in our city an inviting experience.

Our organization was founded by a group of passionate environmentalists who saw the need to expand the tree canopy in our city. We are just beginning our journey to use technology to make Miami greener, and we're excited to partner with you, to help plant trees across our city. Our vision is to create connected, beautiful and sustainable cities for future generations to enjoy.

Our TreePlace App bridges the gap between your desire to plant trees, and the bureaucracy required to do it. You simply click and donate, and we will take care of the rest, the permits, purchase and maintenance of your tree.

TreePlace App facilitates tree planting in three key areas; open spaces (comprised of parks, schools, places of worship and commercial lots), endangered lands, and rights-of-way (the streets that connect our world). We prioritize areas that are most in need, Urban Heat Islands and underserved neighborhoods. By strategically restoring our tree canopy we help reduce air pollution, lower temperatures, and improve overall quality of life for residents. We also educate to help residents understand the importance of trees and how to care for them.

Join us in our mission to make Miami a greener, healthier place to live. Whether you want to volunteer your time, become a corporate sponsor, donate, or simply learn more about the benefits of trees, we welcome you to be a part of our growing community.

Tree of the Month

Native Drought Resistant Wildlife Friendly

Gumbo Limbo

Bursera simaruba

Height: 30-40 ft
Spread: 25-35 ft
Sun: Full to Partial
Water: Low

The Gumbo Limbo, affectionately known as the "Tourist Tree" due to its distinctive red, peeling bark that resembles sunburned tourists, is a beloved native tree of South Florida. This versatile tree has been a vital part of Miami's ecosystem for centuries, known for its rapid growth, beautiful copper-colored bark, and remarkable adaptability to Miami's unique climate.

Historical Significance:

Indigenous peoples used the Gumbo Limbo's resin for various medicinal purposes, earning it the nickname "the healing tree." Its wood was traditionally used for making furniture and canoes, while its bark was used in traditional medicine.

Growing Conditions:

Perfectly suited for Miami's climate, the Gumbo Limbo thrives in our sandy soils and salty conditions. It's particularly well-adapted to coastal areas and can withstand hurricane-force winds, making it an excellent choice for urban landscaping in South Florida.

Benefits:

  • Fast-growing shade tree (can grow up to 3 feet per year)
  • Attracts birds, butterflies, and other wildlife
  • Excellent salt and wind tolerance
  • Low maintenance once established
  • Drought resistant
  • Natural air purifier
  • Helps reduce urban heat island effect

Care Instructions:

While the Gumbo Limbo is relatively low-maintenance, here are some tips for optimal growth:

  • Plant in well-draining soil
  • Water regularly during first year
  • Prune only to remove dead or damaged branches
  • Fertilize lightly in spring and summer
