Derick Phifer

Educational Partner

About Derick

Derick is our key partner at Miami Country Day School, where he plays a crucial role in fostering the next generation of talent for our app's development. He is not only a technical expert but also a dedicated and inspiring educator who is passionate about experiential learning.

By connecting students with this real-world project, Derick ensures a continuous flow of fresh ideas and skills. He helps guarantee that our app remains innovative while providing an invaluable hands-on learning experience for aspiring developers, bridging the gap between the classroom and community impact.

Contributions to TreePlace.App

  • Facilitates the student developer and internship program.
  • Provides mentorship and guidance to our student contributors.
  • Acts as a liaison between TreePlace.App and the educational community.
  • Helps shape the educational outreach components of our mission.
