# TreePlace.App Fact Sheet

## Our Mission
To create a greener, healthier Miami for all residents and visitors by planting trees in urban spaces. We believe in the power of community and technology to make a lasting environmental impact.

## What We Do
- **Tree Planting:** We organize and host tree planting events in parks, schools, and rights-of-way throughout Miami.
- **Community Engagement:** We partner with local residents, businesses, and government to identify areas in need and foster a sense of environmental stewardship.
- **Education:** We provide resources and workshops to educate the community about the importance of urban forests and proper tree care.
- **Technology:** Our app, TreePlace.App, simplifies the process of donating, volunteering, and tracking our collective impact.

## Key Statistics
- **Goal:** To plant 10,000 trees in Miami.
- **Focus Areas:** Urban Heat Islands, underserved neighborhoods, and critical wildlife corridors.
- **Founded:** 2023
