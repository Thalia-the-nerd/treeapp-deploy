Ilyana Albarran

Co-Founder & Head of Operations

About Ilyana

During her time teaching at Miami Country Day school, Ilyana met the talented faculty and students who inspired her to develop the Treeplace.App concept. With a BA in Economics from Cornell and a PhD in Public Administration from FIU, she brings a deep understanding of the bureaucratic and financial hurdles involved in urban environmental projects. Her academic background provides the strategic foundation for our operational success.

As the leader of our non-technical operations, Ilyana is the driving force behind securing partnerships, navigating the permit process, and creating sustainable maintenance routines for every tree planted. Her passion is turning complex civic challenges into simple, actionable steps for the community, ensuring that our green mission is firmly rooted in the realities of urban planning and public policy.

Contributions to TreePlace.App

  • Forged key partnerships with local government and community organizations.
  • Developed the streamlined process for tree planting permits.
  • Manages the budget and financial strategy for sustainable growth.
  • Oversees the long-term care and maintenance plan for all planted trees.
